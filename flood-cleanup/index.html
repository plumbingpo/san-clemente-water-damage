A sump pump that quits, a sewer that backs up, a pipe that lets go, rising river water, storm surge or a hard rain can each flood a property, and the damage should be handled by professionals without delay. Floodwater is almost always rated Category 2 (gray water) or Category 3 (black water), so it carries bacteria, contaminants and possibly hazardous material that can make people seriously ill. For flood emergencies anywhere in San Clemente, San Clemente Restoration is available 24/7 to extract the water, disinfect affected areas and bring the property back safely.

We start with fast water extraction, using truck-mounted and submersible pumps that can move thousands of gallons per hour. Next our technicians, who hold IICRC certification, judge the level of contamination and choose a sanitizing plan that covers HEPA air filtration, antimicrobial application and disinfecting each surface that got wet. We tear out and properly dispose of what cannot be saved, such as soaked carpet padding, contaminated drywall and ruined insulation.

Once the space is extracted and sanitized, our team brings in commercial drying gear, namely low-grain refrigerant (LGR) dehumidifiers plus high-velocity air movers, so the structural components dry all the way through. Daily readings from calibrated moisture meters and a thermal imaging camera tell us when everything is fully dry, which is what keeps mold from growing. Every stage of the job is recorded for your claim: photographs, detailed moisture maps, logs of the equipment used and the scope-of-work reports that insurance adjusters require.

Signs It Is Time for Flood Cleanup

Catching these issues early helps you avoid costly problems and protect your property. Watch for these telltale signs that professional help is needed:

  • A sump pump that failed during heavy rain and let the basement flood
  • Stains along baseboards and lower walls that mark the flood level
  • Rainwater getting inside through a roof or wall damaged by a storm
  • Water coming in through foundation cracks, window wells or door thresholds
  • Any amount of water sitting in a room, garage or basement
  • An overflowing washing machine, dishwasher or water heater
  • A sewer or drain backup pushing contaminated water into living spaces

Flood Cleanup Prevention Tips

Taking preventive measures can help you avoid costly water damage issues down the road. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • Elevate appliances and electrical systems in flood-prone parts of your property
  • Consider flood insurance even if you are not in a designated flood zone
  • Regrade low spots so soil around the whole foundation falls away from the walls
  • Store valuables and important papers off the basement floor in waterproof containers
  • Add a sump pump with battery backup, since power outages and storms tend to arrive together
  • Install backwater valves on sewer lines to stop sewage from backing up into your home

The Benefits of Professional Flood Cleanup

There is much more to a flood cleanup than removing the water you can see. Floodwater carries bacteria, sewage, chemicals and other contaminants that are serious health hazards. Professional flood crews are trained in containment, personal protective equipment (PPE) and sanitizing protocols that keep your family from exposure to pathogens such as E. coli, hepatitis and other waterborne diseases.

Health is not the only issue: when cleanup is done wrong, moisture stays hidden and heavy mold can follow in a matter of days. With thermal imaging, psychrometric calculations and moisture meters, trained technicians confirm that subfloors, structural framing, wall cavities and every other wet area dry to an acceptable standard. This scientific approach to drying is the only reliable way to prevent secondary damage that can cost thousands more to fix.
