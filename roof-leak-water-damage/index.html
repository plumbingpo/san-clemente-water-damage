Dealing With Roof Leak Water Damage in San Clemente

Where you see the stain and where the rain gets in are usually two different places. A loose shingle, a failed flashing joint or an aging seal around a skylight lets rain in, and it travels across the roof deck and framing until it falls into the insulation. Often the attic has been damp for some time before any brown mark reaches the room below. Locating all of the moisture and drying it is our job.

To be clear, roof repair is not what we do, water damage is. Our job is the inside of the house: pulling out soaked insulation, drying the framing and drywall, treating for mold and putting the ceiling back together. When the roof is still letting water through, a temporary tarp is something we can install, and we will coordinate with your roofer so the repair comes before the ceiling is closed.

Your First Steps

  1. Shut Off Wet CircuitsTurn off the breaker for any light, fan or outlet that has water running through or near it, then keep your hands off it.
  2. Contain the WaterPut a pan or bucket beneath each drip, and shift rugs, electronics and furniture clear of the wet area. Plastic or towels spread on the floor will keep it from soaking.
  3. Drain a Bulging CeilingIf the ceiling is holding a pocket of water, stand to one side and poke a small hole at the lowest point so it drains into a bucket.
  4. Stay Off the RoofA wet roof and an unlit attic are both places to stay out of. Just observe the entry point from the room below, then hand the job to a trained crew.
  5. Photograph the DamageUse your phone to record the damaged ceiling, attic and belongings first, hold off on throwing things out until that is done, and call us to get the drying started.
Insurance

Insurance and Roof Leak Water Damage

Interior damage from a sudden, accidental roof leak, like one caused by a fallen limb or wind-torn shingles, is often covered under a homeowners policy. A leak put down to an old, worn or neglected roof is usually denied, and fixing the roof may be treated separately from the interior work. Our team gives your adjuster photos and moisture readings, yet what is covered comes down to your policy, so check it or talk to your agent.

Why It Happens

What Causes It

  • Gaps at chimneys, vents and sidewalls where the flashing has separated or rusted through.
  • Roof valleys where worn metal or piled debris lets water get under the covering.
  • Sun-cracked rubber collars where plumbing vent pipes come through the roof.
  • Missing or broken shingles, often after high wind, with bare underlayment beneath.
  • A fallen limb or large hail that punctures or bruises the roof covering.
Why Speed Matters

What the Water Damages

Ceiling Fixtures and Wiring

Leak water heads for openings in the ceiling, such as recessed lights, fan boxes and junction boxes. Moisture in a fixture can cause a short and later corrosion, which is why it needs inspection before use.

Hidden Attic Mold

Damp framing, paper-faced drywall, warmth and darkness give mold what it needs. Within 24 to 48 hours it can start growing, then move across the bottom of the roof deck.

Rafters and Decking

Rafters, trusses and decking that get wet again and again begin to darken, swell and soften. Rot in these members weakens the structure that holds the roof up.

Drywall Ceilings

Staining, sagging and loss of strength follow when drywall is not dried. The tape releases along cracked seams, and with enough weight a section can come down suddenly.
