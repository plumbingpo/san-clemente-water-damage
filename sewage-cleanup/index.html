Of all the problems an owner can have, sewage coming up through the drains ranks among the most dangerous. Raw sewage contains dangerous bacteria, viruses, parasites and other pathogens, including E. coli, hepatitis, salmonella and giardia, that pose serious health risks to anyone who touches contaminated materials. San Clemente Restoration provides emergency sewage cleanup throughout San Clemente, responding 24/7 with properly trained technicians, appropriate personal protective equipment and EPA-registered disinfectants to decontaminate your property safely.

Water damage comes in categories, and sewage sits in the worst one: Category 3, called black water, the highest level of contamination and danger. This classification requires the most stringent cleanup protocols, including full personal protective equipment, containment barriers, removal of all porous materials that contacted sewage and thorough disinfection of every remaining surface. For Category 3 water damage the reference is IICRC S500, and our crews follow it to bring the building back to safe occupancy.

We clear the standing sewage first, then pull contaminated water out of structural materials, remove carpet, padding, insulation, drywall and any other porous item that was in contact with sewage for proper disposal, disinfect each hard surface, treat with antimicrobial products and finish with structural drying, which prevents secondary mold. To finish, thermal foggers and hydroxyl generators of industrial grade deodorize the space so the sewage smell does not linger.

How to Tell You Need Sewage Cleanup

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Watch for these telltale signs that professional help is needed:

  • People in the household getting stomach or intestinal symptoms with no clear cause
  • Running a sink or washer and hearing a toilet or nearby drain gurgle
  • Dark, dirty water or sewage rising out of a tub, toilet or floor drain
  • Dark residue from sewage left on the basement floor or ringing a drain
  • A cleanout that weeps sewage, or wet foul patches in the lawn along the sewer path
  • Foul-smelling moisture showing up on flooring or along the walls
  • Every sink, tub and shower draining slowly even after you plunge them

Sewage Cleanup Prevention Tips

Taking preventive measures can help you avoid costly water damage issues down the road. Here are our expert recommendations:

  • Toilet paper is the only thing to flush, and that rules out wipes sold as flushable, which still clog
  • Have your sewer line inspected with a camera every 2 to 3 years to catch potential problems
  • A backwater valve at the sewer connection stops the city system from backing up into your home
  • Roots from trees close to the sewer line work their way into pipes and block them, so have them cleared
  • Use drain strainers in showers and sinks to catch hair and debris
  • Keep cooking oil, grease and food scraps out of the drain, where they build into clogs

The Benefits of Professional Sewage Cleanup

Sewage cleanup is not something any homeowner should attempt. The biological hazards in raw sewage require specialized training, proper personal protective equipment (Tyvek suits, respirators, protective gloves and boots) and EPA-registered disinfection products that are not available to consumers. People exposed to the pathogens in sewage can become seriously ill with skin and respiratory infections, hepatitis or gastrointestinal disease.

Disposal matters as well, since everything the sewage contaminated has to be handled as biohazard waste. It is illegal to throw sewage-contaminated insulation, drywall or carpet in the regular trash, and doing so puts the community and the workers who collect it at risk. A certified restoration company follows proper disposal protocols and works with licensed facilities that accept biohazard waste.
